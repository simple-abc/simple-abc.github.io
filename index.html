Our motivation

Some programs we used slowly became less useful to us as features were added. Intrusive ads appeared, required permissions seemed primarily intended to gather data, and accessing files we already owned became harder as we were pushed toward cloud-based services we didn’t want.

We believe developers deserve to be compensated for their work. But sometimes, a monthly subscription seemed unreasonable for what appeared to be a simple app.

We have decided to make simple versions of programs that avoid these kinds of issues.
